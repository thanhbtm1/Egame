📜 GAME RULES 📜

Turn-based & Stealing: Teams will take turns in a circular order. If a team provides an incorrect answer, other teams have the chance to STEAL the turn to answer.

Time Limit: The first team has 20 seconds to provide an answer.
Teams that STEAL the turn will only have 10 seconds to respond.

The "SKIP" Option: If you are stuck or find a question suspicious, you can spend 15 points to SKIP it.

Penalty: Be careful! Choosing the wrong answer will result in a point deduction (-10, -20, or -30 points).

Winning Streak: Successfully answering 3 questions in a row will earn your team a Secret Box.

Life-lines: Each team is granted 3 Life-lines to use throughout the game.

Surprise Elements: Pay close attention to every question—there might be some unexpected surprises waiting for you!

The Grand Prize: The team with the highest score at the end will receive a special gift.
