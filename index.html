📘 Rules

How this version plays

Fixed turnsThe game always cycles Team 1 → Team 2 → Team 3 → Team 4.
Main answerThe current team gets 15 seconds. Correct answer: random $35-$100, +1 ammo, +10 score.
Steal answerIf the main team is wrong, pick one of the other three teams for an 8-second steal.
Reward menuAfter a correct answer, choose Attack, +1 Ammo, Heal 15 HP, or Shop.
Streak crateEvery 3 correct answers in a row unlocks 3 mystery crates. Pick 1, then keep your turn.
Phase 2At 14 tiles left or when any team drops to 150 HP, the game switches to 2 vs 2.
